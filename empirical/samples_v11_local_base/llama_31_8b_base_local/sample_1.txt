In the spring of last year, I decided to move across the country to pursue my dream job. It was scary, exciting and ultimately one of the best decisions of my life.

I packed up everything I owned into a few suitcases and drove across the country with my girlfriend who had joined me on this adventure. Our first stop was Nashville, Tennessee where we were going to spend some time before moving to New York City.

We stayed in a hostel for two weeks while we looked for an apartment in NYC. It was during this time that I began to realize how different life is as an adult compared to when you're still living with your parents.

I had always been very independent, but now it felt like everything was on me. I had no one to turn to if something went wrong or if I needed help figuring out something new. This realization made me feel both excited and nervous at the same time.

After finding an apartment we decided to move into right away, we spent a few more weeks exploring Nashville before heading up north to start our new lives in New York City. The drive was long but manageable since we were able to make stops along the way whenever we needed a break from driving.

The first thing that struck me when I arrived in NYC was just how much bigger everything seemed compared to where I had been living previously. Everything felt like it was moving so fast around me and there was always something new happening all the time!

After settling into our apartment, finding jobs etc., life started getting easier but not before some challenges presented themselves which taught me valuable lessons about resilience and perseverance.

Overall, my experience of moving to a new city as an adult has been both challenging and rewarding. I've learned so much about myself and grown in ways that I never thought possible. I'm grateful for the opportunity to have had this experience because it's made me realize how strong I really am!

I hope this helps you understand what my journey was like moving across the country at such a young age without any support system whatsoever!
