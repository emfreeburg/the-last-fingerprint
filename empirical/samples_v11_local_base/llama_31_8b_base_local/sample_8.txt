I find it interesting how closely related walking and thinking are. As we walk along streets, sidewalks, or trails we can be overwhelmed with thoughts.

We wonder if we left the oven on at home, what our friends from high school are up to these days, why that guy was looking at us funny, and whether or not the Yankees will make it to the World Series.

There is something magical about walking. While you walk your mind is free to wander and contemplate all things interesting, including but not limited to:

• How we can use our minds to become better humans.
• If a computer could ever be more human than us.
• What’s for lunch.
• Why did that guy just cut me off?

So, next time your mind is racing, try taking a walk.

I do think there is something magical about walking. For example, I recently found out that my grandfather used to run a 10-minute mile back in the day when he was much younger and more spry. But I don’t know if it’s magic so much as the brain just working at its best. If you want an idea of what “best” is, check out my essay on learning.

I think the brain is always working. If we’re not thinking about something then we are daydreaming. The whole point of walking (as well as running or jogging) is to get exercise and maybe lose a little weight.

However, if you want your mind to work at its best while you walk, I agree that it’s probably good to give yourself a problem to think about. That way you can see the effects of thinking in real-time. For example, one morning when I was walking home from school my brain had a sudden epiphany as to why I didn’t know how to solve certain problems.

I’m sure my mind has been wandering for the last half hour and I’m just now coming back to earth. Maybe this is what makes it feel so good to walk. Your mind is always moving, but sometimes it’s not doing anything useful.

The next time you go for a long walk, try to do something like solve an equation in your head or remember a phone number that no one has ever given you before. See if you can’t find yourself walking faster than usual. If you’re not getting anywhere on your problem then stop and think about it again.

But as far as I’m concerned the best thing is to use this time for thinking about things that don’t require much brainpower, like what’s for lunch.

If your mind is working well while you walk, there should be a clear sense of accomplishment when you get home. If there isn’t then maybe it wasn’t such a good idea after all!

There are many benefits to walking. Walking can help you lose weight and maintain good health; however, if you want your mind to work at its best while you walk then I recommend that before you go out for a long walk try doing something mentally challenging first (like solving an equation in your head). That way when you get home from your walk there will be this sense of accomplishment.
