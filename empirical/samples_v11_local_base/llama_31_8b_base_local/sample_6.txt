Airports are a peculiar place. They're not really on the ground or in the sky, but somewhere in-between. That's what makes them such an interesting space to write about. Airports are filled with people who are all going through similar experiences: they've all just left their homes and will soon be landing at their destination. There is something comforting about knowing that you're not alone on your journey.

This essay looks at airports as a place of transit, a liminal zone where travelers are neither here nor there but somewhere in-between. It explores the emotional impact of being in such a unique space and what it means to be a stranger in a foreign land.
