I want to live in a neighborhood that feels like home. I don't mean the kind of place where everyone knows your name and you can borrow sugar from the neighbor down the street. A place where people have been living for generations, so that even if you moved away and came back 20 years later, many things would be just as you remembered.

But I think it's possible to create these kinds of neighborhoods without sacrificing progress or innovation. That's what makes a neighborhood feel like home to me.
