Why do we enjoy storms? I’m not sure if it’s the destruction that appeals to us so much. Watching buildings collapse, cars flip over and entire neighborhoods get flooded may seem like an awful thing but there’s something fascinating about seeing nature take its course. Perhaps it's because it reminds us of how weak we are when compared with such a force.

Watching storms has always been a hobby for me. As long as I can remember, I’ve spent hours upon hours sitting in front of the television screen watching tornadoes rip through homes or hurricanes battering coastal cities. There’s something peaceful about watching these natural disasters unfold before your eyes. It makes you feel insignificant but at the same time it helps you appreciate life more because you know that no matter what happens, there will always be someone out there who has experienced worse than us.

I find myself drawn to storms for many reasons beyond just their destructive power. For one thing, they offer up a unique opportunity to learn about science and physics in action as tornados form from warm air rising while hurricanes gain strength from the heat energy stored within them. The complexity of these systems can be overwhelming but also incredibly interesting once you start understanding how everything fits together. Plus, there’s something cathartic about witnessing nature at its most raw form - it gives me a sense of relief to know that despite whatever chaos is going on around us, eventually everything will return back to normal again.

Finally, watching storms brings people closer together because they provide an exciting shared experience between strangers who otherwise wouldn’t interact with each other. It’s amazing how quickly conversations start up when two random individuals find themselves glued to the same television screen during a major storm event. This communal connection is something I believe we need more of these days given our increasingly isolated lives spent staring at screens instead of talking face-to-face with others.

So whether you’re into it for the science, the spectacle or simply because watching people get swept away by nature feels like watching someone escape reality temporarily, there are plenty of reasons to appreciate storms. After all, they serve as powerful reminders that no matter what happens in life, we’ll always have something to watch!
